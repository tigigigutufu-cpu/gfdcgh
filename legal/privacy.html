Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ToolSphere.online ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use our website and tools. We built ToolSphere to work without accounts, and most tools process your input entirely in your browser without sending it to our servers.

1. Information we collect

We do not require account creation and do not collect personal information to let you use our core tools. We may automatically collect standard, non-identifying technical data such as browser type, device type, approximate location (country/city level), and pages visited, typically through analytics and advertising partners described below.

2. How our tools handle your input

Most tools (calculators, generators, converters) run entirely client-side in JavaScript — your text, numbers or files never leave your device. A small number of tools (the QR Code Generator and AI Image Generator) send your input to a third-party public API to render a result; we do not store this input ourselves, but the third-party provider's own privacy practices apply to that request.

3. Cookies and advertising

We may use cookies and similar technologies for analytics and to display advertising, including personalised ads served by third-party ad networks (such as Google AdSense). These partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can control cookies through your browser settings. See our Cookie Policy for more detail.

4. Contact form and email

If you email us or use our contact form, we receive whatever information you choose to share (such as your name and email address) solely to respond to your message. We do not sell this information.

5. Children's privacy

ToolSphere is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Our learning tools are designed for general use by students but do not require any personal information to use.

6. Third-party links and services

Our site may link to or use third-party services (image and QR generation endpoints, font providers, ad networks). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties — please review their own policies.

7. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect the limited data we do handle, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email toolsphereweb@gmail.com.
