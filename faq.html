Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about using ToolSphere.

Is ToolSphere really free?

Yes. All core tools on ToolSphere are free to use with no signup or hidden paywall. We support the site through non-intrusive ads.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required for any tool. Just open a tool page and start using it.

Is my data stored or shared?

Most tools run entirely in your browser, so your input never leaves your device. A few tools (like the QR and image generator) call a public third-party endpoint to render results — see each tool's FAQ for details.

How does the AI Image Generator keep content safe?

Every prompt passes through a client-side filter that blocks explicit, sexual, violent or hateful requests before any image is generated, and we automatically add modest, respectful styling to every prompt.

Can I suggest a new tool?

Yes! Email us at toolsphereweb@gmail.com with your idea and we'll consider it for a future update.

Do the finance calculators give exact figures from my bank?

No, they provide estimates based on standard formulas. Always confirm exact figures with your bank or financial institution.

Is ToolSphere available on mobile?

Yes, every tool is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets and desktops.

How do I report a bug?

Email toolsphereweb@gmail.com with details of the issue and we'll look into it.
