About this tool

Upload a photo, then drag it inside the circular preview to position your face exactly where you want and use the zoom slider to scale it. Pick a category — Country Flags, Styles & Badges, or Colors & Gradients — and choose a frame to see it applied instantly. Download a high-resolution PNG ready for WhatsApp, Instagram, or LinkedIn. Everything runs in your browser, so your photo is never uploaded anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Is the DP maker free to use?

Yes, it's completely free with no signup or watermark on the download.

Can I move my photo inside the frame?

Yes — click or touch and drag inside the circular preview to reposition your photo, and use the zoom slider to scale it. A reset button recenters it instantly.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. Your photo is processed entirely in your browser using the Canvas API — it is never uploaded anywhere.

What format can I download my profile picture in?

You get a high-resolution 1000x1000 PNG file, ready to use on WhatsApp, Instagram, or LinkedIn.

Can I combine a flag frame with a badge frame?

You can apply one frame at a time — selecting a new flag, style, or color frame replaces whichever one was applied before.
