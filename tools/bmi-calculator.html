About this tool

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple screening measure of weight relative to height. It's a general indicator only — it doesn't account for muscle mass, bone density, age or body composition.

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No, BMI doesn't distinguish muscle from fat, so it can be less accurate for athletes, older adults, or during pregnancy.

What is a healthy BMI range?

18.5 to 24.9 is generally considered a normal weight range for most adults.
