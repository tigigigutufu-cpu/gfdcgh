Ever seen an AI-generated image or a cool video frame and wanted to know what prompt might recreate it? Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI Prompt Extractor work?

Upload an image, or a video (we automatically grab a frame from it), and our AI vision engine analyzes the subject, style, lighting, and composition to write a ready-to-use text prompt.

Is this prompt extractor free to use?

Yes, it's completely free with no signup and no API key required. It runs on free AI vision providers behind the scenes.

Can I extract a prompt from a video file?

Yes. When you upload a video, your browser automatically captures a representative frame and sends that frame to the AI for description -- no video is ever uploaded to a server.
