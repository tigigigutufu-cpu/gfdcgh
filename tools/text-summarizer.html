About this tool

This tool analyzes your pasted text, scores each sentence by how central it is to the overall content, and pulls out the most important sentences to form a short summary — useful for quickly digesting long articles, research papers, or reports.

How to use this tool

  1. Paste your article or long text into the input box.
  2. Choose how many sentences you want in your summary.
  3. Click Summarize to instantly get the key sentences pulled out.

Frequently asked questions

How does the summarization work?

It uses an extractive algorithm that identifies the most important sentences in your text based on word frequency and relevance, then presents them in original order.

Is there a text length limit?

Very long text (multiple pages) works best when summarized in smaller chunks for more focused results.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No, all processing happens locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

Does it work for languages other than English?

It's optimized for English text; results for other languages may be less accurate.
