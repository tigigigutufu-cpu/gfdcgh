About this tool

Combine two or more PDF files into a single document securely within your browser. Files are never uploaded to a server.

How to use this tool

  1. Click 'Browse Files' or drag and drop your PDF files.
  2. Use the up/down buttons to reorder files if needed.
  3. Click Merge PDFs and download your combined file.

Frequently asked questions

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No, all merging happens locally in your browser.

Can I select multiple files?

Yes, you can select multiple files at once or add more one by one.

Will the merged PDF have a watermark?

No, the output is completely clean and watermark-free.

Is there a file size limit?

It depends on your browser and device memory.
