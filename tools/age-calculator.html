About this tool

Calculate your exact age in years, months and days as of today or any custom date — useful for forms, eligibility checks and fun facts like total days lived.

Frequently asked questions

Can I calculate age as of a past or future date?

Yes, use the 'Calculate age as of' field to pick any date.

Does it account for leap years?

Yes, the calculation uses exact calendar dates, so leap years are automatically handled correctly.

Can I calculate the age of someone else, like a child?

Yes, just enter their date of birth — the tool works for anyone's birth date.

Is this useful for eligibility or form purposes?

Yes, many forms (school admissions, job applications, government IDs) ask for exact age, which this tool calculates precisely.

How to use this tool

  1. Enter your date of birth in the first field.
  2. Optionally set a different 'calculate age as of' date, or leave it as today.
  3. Click Calculate to instantly see your exact age in years, months and days.
