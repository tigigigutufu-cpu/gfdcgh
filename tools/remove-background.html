About this tool

Upload a photo with a clear main subject and pick a replacement background — great for profile photos, ID-style pictures and product shots. The AI keeps your subject exactly as it is and swaps only the background. A small "TP" watermark is added to the result.

Frequently asked questions

Does this give a transparent PNG?

The result is a clean solid-color background (white, black, gray or blue), not a pixel-level transparent cutout — ideal for ID photos, profile pictures and simple product shots.

What kind of photos work best?

Photos with one clear subject (a person or a product) against a reasonably simple background work best.

Is my photo stored anywhere?

No, your photo is sent securely to the AI provider for processing and is not stored on our server.
