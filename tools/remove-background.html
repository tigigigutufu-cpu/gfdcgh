About this tool

Upload a photo with a clear main subject and pick a replacement background — transparent, white, black, gray, blue, or any custom color of your choice. Great for profile photos, ID-style pictures and product shots. All output images are 100% clean with zero watermarks.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a transparent PNG?

Yes — choose "Transparent (no background)" from the dropdown to get a true transparent-background PNG.

What kind of photos work best?

Photos with one clear subject (a person or a product) against a reasonably plain background work best.

Are there any watermarks on the result?

No, all output images are completely clean without any added logos or watermarks.
