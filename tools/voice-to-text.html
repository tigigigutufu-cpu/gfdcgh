About this tool

This tool uses your browser's built-in speech recognition to convert spoken words into text live, as you speak — useful for quick notes, dictating messages, or drafting content hands-free. Everything happens in your browser; no audio is uploaded to our servers.

How to use this tool

  1. Select your preferred language (English or Hindi).
  2. Click "Start Speaking" and allow microphone access when prompted.
  3. Speak clearly — your words appear as text in real time.
  4. Click Stop when done, then Copy Text to use it elsewhere.

Frequently asked questions

Which browsers support this?

Chrome and Edge have the best support for browser-based speech recognition. Safari and some other browsers may have limited or no support.

Is my voice recorded or stored?

No, the audio is processed by your browser's built-in recognition engine and is not saved or uploaded to our servers.

Does it support languages other than English and Hindi?

Currently English (India/US) and Hindi are supported; more languages may be added over time.

Why isn't it picking up my speech accurately?

Background noise, unclear pronunciation, or a low-quality microphone can affect accuracy. Try speaking slowly and clearly in a quiet environment.
