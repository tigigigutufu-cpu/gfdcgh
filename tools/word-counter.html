About this tool

A live word, character, sentence and paragraph counter with estimated reading time (based on 200 words per minute) — useful for essays, captions, meta descriptions and video scripts.

Frequently asked questions

Does this tool save my text?

No, everything happens locally in your browser and nothing is uploaded or stored.

How is reading time calculated?

We use an average adult reading speed of 200 words per minute.
