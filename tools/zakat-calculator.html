About this tool

Zakat is calculated as 2.5% of your total zakatable wealth (cash, gold, silver, savings and business assets) held for one lunar year, above the Nisab threshold, after deducting immediate debts. This tool totals your assets, subtracts debts, and applies the 2.5% rate automatically.

How to use this tool

  1. Enter the current value of your cash, gold, silver, and business assets.
  2. Enter any short-term debts you owe, which are deducted from your total wealth.
  3. Click Calculate Zakat to see your total zakatable wealth and the 2.5% Zakat amount due.

Frequently asked questions

What is Nisab?

Nisab is the minimum threshold of wealth (commonly based on the value of 87.48g gold or 612.36g silver) above which Zakat becomes obligatory. Check the current Nisab value with a trusted local source before finalizing.

Is the rate always 2.5%?

Yes, for cash, gold, silver, savings and business assets held for a full lunar year, the standard Zakat rate is 2.5%.

Should I deduct my debts before calculating?

Yes, immediate, short-term debts are typically deducted from your total wealth before applying the 2.5% Zakat rate.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No, all calculations happen locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved.
